How a job goes

You call or text

Tell Emerson what you want done. He sets a time to come and look, usually within the week.

We come and measure

We look behind the vanity, under the floor and up at the ceiling, so the quote has no surprises in it.

Your written quote

Every job on paper, with the price, the materials and the days it will take.

Tear-out day

Floors and furniture covered, the old room comes out and leaves in our truck.

Plumbing goes in

New supply lines, valves and drains while the walls are still open.

Tile and floors

Backer board, waterproofing, then tile and planks laid level.

Paint and trim

Walls patched and painted, baseboards and casings back on.

The walkthrough

We walk the room with you and fix anything before we call it done.

Questions

Is the estimate really free? Yes. We come out, measure and send a written quote at no cost, and you owe nothing if you don't book.

What areas do you work in? Marietta and the towns around it in Cobb County and metro Atlanta. [Confirm the service radius.]

Do you do the whole bathroom, or just the tile? The whole job: tear-out, plumbing, tile, floor, paint and fixtures, with one crew from start to finish.

Will different crews show up on different days? No. The same people who tear out your bathroom set the tile and paint the walls.

How long does a job take? A repair visit is usually a day or less, a room refresh two to four days, and a full bathroom one to two weeks. Your quote has the schedule in it.

Can I buy my own tile or flooring? Yes. We will tell you how much to order and check it before we start, or we can source it for you.

Do I need to move out? Rarely. We work one room at a time, cover what stays and clean up every evening.
